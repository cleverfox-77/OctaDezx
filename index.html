AI phone calls

OctaDezx answers the phone. A business gets its own real number and the AI agent picks it up, holds an ordinary spoken conversation, and can be interrupted mid-sentence exactly like a person: cut across it and it stops talking and listens. It is not a phone menu and there is nothing to press. It answers from the same catalogue, prices, policies and opening hours the chat uses, so it can never quote a price the website has changed.

  • Inbound: answers a real number in real time, with barge-in, so a caller can interrupt it mid-sentence
  • Takes orders and appointment requests on the call and files them into the dashboard for the owner to confirm
  • Hands over to a person, or takes a voicemail and transcribes it, when it cannot help
  • Outbound: appointment reminders and follow-ups dialled on a schedule, each opening with a spoken opt-out
  • Every call is written into the same conversation history as the WhatsApp and Instagram threads, as plain text
  • After a call it can send the details on WhatsApp, when the caller has messaged that business from the same number within Meta's 24-hour window
  • It never claims to be a human. Asked, it says plainly that it is an automated assistant. The wording is configurable, the disclosure is not
  • Included minutes: Starter 100 a month, Pro 400, Advanced 900, Enterprise metered at $0.12 a minute with no cap
